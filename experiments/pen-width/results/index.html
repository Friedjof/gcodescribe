Pen width and plot optimisation

Reproducible planning demo. All lengths are in millimetres, using synthetic artwork and a round, constant-width pen. Images are enlarged comparisons, not a physical measurement scale.

This demo does not change the app. It measures geometric ink coverage and calculated times. Paper, ink deposition and actual machine motion are not simulated.

Why spacing alone is insufficient

With a 0.4 mm pen and 0.12 mm line spacing, the second centreline lies inside the first stroke's coverage. Removing it still loses 23.12% of the combined ink area. A percentage budget alone could also remove an entire punctuation mark; here that represents just 0.00626%.
